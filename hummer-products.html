Can You Fly With HUMMER Products?

Yes. Most of the HUMMER range sits under the 100Wh IATA threshold, which means it travels in your carry-on baggage.

Rules you must follow

  • Portable chargers must be carried in your cabin baggage, never in checked luggage.
  • Each portable charger must not exceed 100Wh (watt-hours).
  • Check with your airline before you pack them.

Calculating watt-hours

Watt-hours are milliamp-hours divided by 1,000, multiplied by voltage.

(mAh ÷ 1000) × V = Wh

A 10,000mAh power bank at 3.7V works out to 37Wh, comfortably under the 100Wh limit.

Rated on every unit

Each HUMMER product carries its watt-hour rating printed on the back of the unit, so there is nothing to work out at the airport.

Most of the range falls under 100Wh and meets IATA requirements for carriage on flights.

If a unit is over 100Wh

Units rated 100.1–160Wh may be carried in limited numbers with prior airline approval. Anything above 160Wh is not permitted on passenger aircraft.

Always confirm with your airline before you travel.

Guidance shown is based on IATA lithium battery rules and is provided for reference only. Airline and civil aviation requirements vary by carrier and route — confirm with your airline before you fly.
