About the Company

A Dedicated Agency for Principal Representation

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. operates as a separate, structured company focused on commercial agency, local trading coordination, and authorized representative services for global manufacturers in Sri Lanka.

Corporate Standing

Built for Formal, Reliable Commercial Handling

We provide a professional local point of contact for contractors, engineers, and supply chain managers seeking official access to represented brands. We facilitate quotations, document routing, technical evaluations, and compliance coordination directly between the local buyer and the international manufacturer.

Governance

Separate Legal Entity

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. is registered as an independent trading company. It shares the signature Neptune brand visual identity to maintain corporate cohesion, but its website, operations, and services are distinct from logistics and shipping business arms.

Core Competency

Our focus lies strictly in authorized sales agency and commercial product supply representation. By specializing, we ensure dedicated attention to brand catalogues, SKU-level updates, and responsive commercial follow-ups.

Operating Guidelines

Core Professional Principles

We govern our day-to-day operations with a strict code of professional conduct to keep relationships transparent and orders smooth.

Clarity

Transparent Communication

We route all enquiries, tech specs, and quotes with maximum accuracy to prevent errors and ensure both buyers and manufacturers align perfectly.

Ethics

Brand Integrity

Third-party logos and marks are treated with strict compliance, representing authorized portfolios with total respect for patent and trademark guidelines.

Timeliness

Operational Speed

We understand project timelines. Enquiry packages are processed, catalog queries resolved, and emails routed with the highest commercial responsiveness.

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. is a dedicated, separate trading entity representing selected international principals in Sri Lanka.
