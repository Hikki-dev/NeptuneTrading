About Neptune Trading

Founded in Sri Lanka. Built for global trade.

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. was established in 1984 as a dedicated trading and export entity. Originally founded by Mohomed Ansar, the company has established a strong legacy of trust and trade partnerships spanning over four decades. Today, the organization is directed by Inam Ul Hassan, Managing Director and son of the founder, who oversees Neptune's global trade relations, principal representations, and modern operations under a unified focus on commercial accountability and structured follow-up.
