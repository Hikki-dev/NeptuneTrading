About the Company

A dedicated Sri Lankan trading and export company.

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. was founded in 2010 and is structured to support export trade, sourcing, procurement, and market development with clear commercial communication and disciplined enquiry handling.

Corporate Position

Independent focus. Shared Neptune standards.

The company shares the Neptune visual identity and professional standards while remaining focused on tea exports, spices exports, fibre exports, sourcing, procurement, and export market coordination.

What We Do

We provide a formal point of contact for customers and commercial partners seeking export supply, supplier coordination, market-entry support, quotation coordination, and structured follow-up.
