From ship handling origins to modern commercial trade.

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. was established in 1984 as a dedicated trading and export entity. Originally founded by Mohomed Ansar following his launch of Neptune Supply & Co in 1982, the firm has established a strong legacy of trust and trade partnerships spanning over four decades. Today, the organization is continued by Inam Ul Hassan, Managing Director and son of the founder, carrying forward the family-led legacy with modern operations, authorized global principal representations, and rigorous trading desks.

Common Questions

When was Neptune Trading Company founded?

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd was founded in 1984 by Mohomed Ansar, building on the maritime foundation he established with Neptune Supply & Co in 1982. The company has maintained continuous commercial operations from Colombo for over 40 years.

Who leads Neptune Trading Company today?

Neptune Trading Company is currently led by Inam Ul Hassan, Managing Director and son of the founder Mohomed Ansar. Under his leadership the company has expanded into global principal representation, authorized distributorships, and structured procurement coordination.

What products does Neptune Trading Company represent?

Neptune Trading is the authorized distributor for HUMMER Power Products in Sri Lanka & Maldives (GM-licensed jump starters and power solutions), and Snow / Aliaxis Piping Systems and Metal Alloys Corporation in Sri Lanka.

Where is Neptune Trading Company based?

Our head office is at 66B Vivekananda Hill, Kotahena, Colombo 13, Sri Lanka, with a city office at 344/17 Jampettah Street, Colombo 01300. All commercial enquiries are handled through our Colombo trading desk at info@neptunetrading.lk or 0117 99 11 05.
