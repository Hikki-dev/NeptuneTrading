# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three overlapping audiences:
1. **B2B buyers and procurement teams** — contractors, MEP engineers, fleet operators, hospital and institutional buyers evaluating whether to source through Neptune. They arrive with a specific product category in mind and need to quickly confirm Neptune is the authorized, credible channel.
2. **International suppliers seeking local representation** — foreign manufacturers looking for a Sri Lankan distribution partner. They need to understand Neptune's track record, reach, and commercial credibility.
3. **General brand credibility** — stakeholders, referrals, and industry contacts checking Neptune out. The site must establish instant authority.

All three need the same thing fast: "this is a serious, established, active trading operation."

## Product Purpose

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd is Sri Lanka's authorized distributor for HUMMER Power Products (Sri Lanka and Maldives) and Metal Alloys Corporation. Founded 1984. The website's job is to convert qualified B2B visitors — either into product enquiries or into an understanding of what Neptune does well enough to start a commercial conversation.

Success: a procurement manager or international supplier spends 2–3 minutes on the site and leaves knowing exactly what Neptune offers, feeling confident in their credibility, and knowing how to reach them.

## Brand Personality

**Established. Sharp. Trusted.**

Voice: direct, professional, no marketing fluff. The copy should read like a well-run Colombo commercial desk — confident, specific, not trying to impress with adjectives.

Emotional goal: make visitors feel like they've found the right partner — not a directory listing, not a startup, but a company that has been doing this for decades and knows exactly what it's doing.

## Anti-references

- Cheap local business directories or template-heavy Sri Lankan corporate sites — no clip-art, no low-resolution logos, no bloated footer columns
- Generic SaaS/startup aesthetic — no hero metric cards, no gradient text, no "feature grid" layouts, no floating blobs
- Nautical clichés — no anchors, no rope textures, no literal ship iconography used decoratively
- Overly cold or sterile minimalism — white space alone does not equal premium; the site must feel active and inhabited
- Dark-mode-as-default — the site should be predominantly light, readable, user-friendly; dark sections as accents only

## Design Principles

1. **Authority through specificity** — every section names real things: actual product lines, real territory (Sri Lanka, Maldives), real year (1984). No vague claims.
2. **Blue as identity, not decoration** — the Neptune navy-to-blue range is the brand. Use it boldly and purposefully: hero backgrounds, section accents, CTA hierarchy. Not as decoration everywhere.
3. **Show the product divisions clearly** — HUMMER and Metal Alloys are the core product representation lines. They must feel like distinct, real things — not generic cards.
4. **Motion earns its place** — animations should make the site feel like a living operation, not add friction. Scroll reveals are tasteful, not theatrical. Counters, subtle parallax, and entrance transitions only where they reinforce credibility.
5. **One clear path per visitor type** — buyers go to product divisions; suppliers go to contact; browsers go to What We Do. Navigation and CTAs must reflect this.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA as minimum. Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast against background.
- Reduced motion alternative for all scroll and entrance animations.
- Touch-safe hover interactions (pointer media query guards).
- Legible at all viewport sizes — no heading overflow on mobile.
