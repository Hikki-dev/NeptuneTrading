Key Properties & Advantages

Free-cutting grades (C36000) offer the highest machinability rating in the brass family

DZR / Eco Brass grades meet the most stringent lead-free plumbing regulations

High-tensile manganese brass grades provide strength for stressed mechanical parts

Wide profile range — hex, square, hollow — suits automatic lathe and CNC machining

Leaded hollow rods dimensionally stable for bushing and bearing applications

Forging grades (C37700) hot-work readily with excellent die-fill characteristics
