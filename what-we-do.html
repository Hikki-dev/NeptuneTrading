Commercial Operations

Core Sourcing, Representation & Export Pillars

Neptune Trading Company operates as a highly specialized commercial broker and sourcing firm. We connect global manufacturers with domestic industrial needs, facilitate national public-sector bidding, and run high-efficiency export channels for Sri Lanka's premium commodities.

How We Work

Clear process. Accountable response.

Whether the requirement is an imported product, authorized principal enquiry, export category, sourcing brief, or market-entry discussion, every enquiry follows a disciplined commercial path from submission to coordinated response.

Start Your Enquiry
01

Submit Your Requirement

Share the product category, principal brand if relevant, destination market, quantity, quality expectations, timing, and documentation requirements.

02

Commercial Review

Our desk confirms the trading scope, identifies the correct import, export, sourcing, or market-entry route, and clarifies required compliance steps.

03

Principal or Supplier Coordination

The enquiry is coordinated with the relevant manufacturer, supplier, export channel, or market contact for a structured commercial response.

04

Coordinated Commercial Response

Pricing, availability, documentation, market context, and quotation details are returned through Neptune Trading Company via a clear communication channel.

Trading Infrastructure

Three Strategic Business Divisions

Operating with transactional transparency, regulatory accuracy, and standard compliance across three principal spheres of global commercial supply.

Division I

Strategic Sourcing & Procurement

We function as the primary local sourcing desk for corporate entities and major engineering projects requiring high-integrity industrial inputs. We eliminate supplier friction by vetting international fabricators, verifying compliance certificates, and conducting structured price negotiation.

  • ✓
    Global Manufacturer Identification: Vetting international factories against specific mechanical tolerances and volume parameters.
  • ✓
    Compliance & Quality Audits: Matching physical supply credentials to EN, ISO, BS, and ASTM standards prior to shipping.
  • ✓
    Commercial Contract Negotiation: Brokering letters of credit (LC), trade financing timelines, and customized bulk volume pricing.

Division II

Export Development & Supply Chain

Sri Lanka's premium commodities command global market demand. Neptune Trading coordinates estate procurement, standard chemical analysis clearance, customs documentation, and containerized dispatch for traditional export items, with CEC Commercial Export Company supporting tea, spices, fibre, and agricultural export supply requirements.

  • ✓
    CEC Export Supply Support: Coordinating pure Ceylon Tea packaging, standard blends, fibre, spices, and direct bulk export options (cecteas.com).
  • ✓
    Commodity Exports: Sourcing high-oil Ceylon Cinnamon, pure cloves, black pepper, and high-tensile coconut coir fibre for industrial farming.
  • ✓
    Customs & Quality Control: Handling phytosanitary certificates, Chamber of Commerce approvals, and strict SGS audits.
  • ✓
    Shipment Execution Support: Freight forwarding, customs clearance, port handling, warehousing, and inland transport coordination supported by Neptune Logistics where required.

Division III

Market Entry & Commercial Agency

Foreign industrial manufacturers seeking to participate in Sri Lanka's public infrastructure bids and utility contracts require a connected, legally robust regional agent. We act as your localized trading desk, representing your brand in front of project decision makers, coordinating bid submissions, and establishing distribution channels.

  • ✓
    Public Sector Tendering: Navigation of government tender boards, ministry specs registration, and structured commercial bid delivery.
  • ✓
    Regulatory & Import Management: Handling import licensing, Sri Lanka Standards (SLS) certification, and port clearances.
  • ✓
    Localized Account Representation: Regular commercial channel management and direct marketing of represented lines (Hummer, Aliaxis, Metal Alloys).
