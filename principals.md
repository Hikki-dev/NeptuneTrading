## What Is Principal Representation?

Principal representation means Neptune Trading Company acts as the official commercial agent for an international manufacturer in Sri Lanka — managing enquiries, bid coordination, compliance documentation, and commercial supply on the principal's behalf.

#### For Sri Lankan Buyers

You deal with a local commercial desk that understands your project requirements, can coordinate technical documentation, and routes your enquiry directly to the authorized principal. No grey-market sourcing, no supply chain uncertainty.

#### For International Principals

Your brand is represented by a locally established trading company with over 40 years of commercial experience in Colombo — handling market intelligence, regulatory compliance, tender participation, and buyer relationships on your behalf.

#### Authorized Supply Chain

All products handled through Neptune Trading come through a formally agreed agency arrangement. Buyers receive genuine products with appropriate documentation, and principals retain full control over brand standards and pricing policy.

#### Structured Bid Coordination

For government and institutional tenders, Neptune Trading coordinates technical submissions, compliance certificates, mill test certificates, and commercial bid documents — ensuring your product specification reaches the right decision makers.
