What is Neptune Trading Company?

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan B2B trading, sourcing, and procurement company established in Colombo in 1984. The company acts as the authorized local distributor and commercial representative for selected international manufacturers including HUMMER Power Products (for Sri Lanka & Maldives) and Metal Alloys Corporation. Neptune Trading coordinates product sourcing, procurement support, export development, and market-entry assistance for businesses, institutions, and international suppliers from its Colombo commercial desk.

Who Should Contact Us

Importers, exporters, MEP contractors, fleet operators, hospital procurement teams, construction companies, industrial manufacturers, and international suppliers seeking Sri Lankan market entry.

What We Supply

GM-licensed HUMMER portable power products and Metal Alloys Corporation copper, brass, and bronze industrial alloys — all through an officially authorized supply chain.

How to Enquire

Submit your product requirement, quantity, industry application, and delivery location via our contact form or email info@neptunetrading.lk. Commercial desk responds to all qualified B2B enquiries.

What does Neptune Trading Company do?

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan B2B trading company established in 1984. We act as the authorized local distributor and commercial representative for selected international product principals, and coordinate sourcing, procurement, and export support from our Colombo desk.

What product divisions does Neptune Trading represent?

We represent two authorized product divisions: HUMMER Power Products (jump starters, power stations, power banks) and Metal Alloys Corporation (copper, brass, and bronze alloys).

Is Neptune Trading Company an authorized distributor?

Yes. Neptune Trading holds formal agency and distributorship agreements for HUMMER Power Products (authorized for Sri Lanka & Maldives, General Motors licensed) and Metal Alloys Corporation. All products are sourced through an officially authorized supply chain.
