What We Do

Two commercial pillars. One accountable trading desk.

Import and Distribution Representation

We bring selected international products into Sri Lanka.

As authorized distributors for appointed manufacturers, Neptune Trading coordinates quotations, technical documentation, supplier communication, compliance support, and commercial follow-up for Sri Lankan customers.

  • Hummer machinery, equipment, and spare parts enquiries
  • Aliaxis piping systems, conduits, fittings, and valves
  • Metal Alloys Corporation steel, alloys, and industrial materials
  • Supplier verification, bid coordination, and documentation
Export Operations

We coordinate Sri Lankan products for global markets.

Our export activity focuses on Ceylon tea, spices, and fibre, supported by buyer communication, sourcing discipline, pre-shipment coordination, and destination-market documentation.

  • Ceylon tea exports to the Middle East and South Asia
  • Spices exports to Malaysia, India, Singapore, and Dubai
  • Fibre export coordination for China and Far East markets
  • Quality expectations, pricing, and shipment follow-up
