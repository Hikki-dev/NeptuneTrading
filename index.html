About Neptune Trading

What is Neptune Trading Company?

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan B2B trading, sourcing, and procurement company established in Colombo in 1984. The company acts as the authorized local distributor and commercial representative for selected international manufacturers including HUMMER Power Products (for Sri Lanka & Maldives), Snow / Aliaxis Piping Systems, and Metal Alloys Corporation. Neptune Trading coordinates product sourcing, procurement support, export development, and market-entry assistance for businesses, institutions, and international suppliers from its Colombo commercial desk.

Who Should Contact Us

Importers, exporters, MEP contractors, fleet operators, hospital procurement teams, construction companies, industrial manufacturers, and international suppliers seeking Sri Lankan market entry.

What We Supply

GM-licensed HUMMER portable power products, Snow/Aliaxis LESSO brand industrial piping systems, and Metal Alloys Corporation copper, brass, and bronze industrial alloys — all through an officially authorized supply chain.

How to Enquire

Submit your product requirement, quantity, industry application, and delivery location via our contact form or email info@neptunetrading.lk. Commercial desk responds to all qualified B2B enquiries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about Neptune Trading Company, our product divisions, and how to work with us.

What does Neptune Trading Company do?

Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd is a Sri Lankan B2B trading company established in 1984. We act as the authorized local distributor and commercial representative for selected international product principals, and coordinate sourcing, procurement, and export support from our Colombo desk.

Who should contact Neptune Trading Company?

Importers, exporters, contractors, MEP engineers, industrial procurement teams, fleet operators, institutional buyers, and international suppliers seeking Sri Lankan market representation should contact us. We handle B2B, institutional, and bulk enquiries.

What product divisions does Neptune Trading represent?

We represent three authorized product divisions: HUMMER Power Products (jump starters, power stations, power banks), Snow / Aliaxis Piping Systems (industrial and commercial piping), and Metal Alloys Corporation (copper, brass, and bronze alloys).

Is Neptune Trading Company an authorized distributor?

Yes. Neptune Trading holds formal agency and distributorship agreements for HUMMER Power Products (authorized for Sri Lanka & Maldives, General Motors licensed), Snow / Aliaxis Piping Systems (LESSO Snow brand), and Metal Alloys Corporation. All products are sourced through an officially authorized supply chain — no grey-market sourcing.

Does Neptune Trading sell directly to consumers?

We primarily serve businesses, institutions, and commercial buyers. We handle B2B product enquiries, bulk procurement, fleet supply, and project-based requirements. Enquiries should include product required, quantity, application, and company details for our desk to respond appropriately.

How can I submit a product or sourcing enquiry?

Use our contact form or reach us directly at info@neptunetrading.lk or 0117 99 11 05. Please include the product required, quantity, industry or application, delivery location, and your company name for a structured commercial response.

What is principal representation in trading?

Principal representation means a local trading company acts as the official commercial agent for an international manufacturer in a specific market. Neptune Trading represents selected international product manufacturers as their authorized Sri Lankan partner, handling local enquiries, bid coordination, compliance documentation, and supply chain routing on their behalf. This gives buyers a reliable, locally accountable source for internationally branded products.

Can international suppliers partner with Neptune Trading?

Yes. International manufacturers seeking Sri Lankan market representation can contact Neptune Trading through our trading desk. We evaluate new principal partnerships based on product category, market fit, compliance standards, and commercial viability. Interested suppliers should include their company profile, product range, certifications, and target market requirement in their initial enquiry.
