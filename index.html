Who We Are

A dedicated trading entity. Built for export-led commerce.

Founded in 2010, Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. operates as an independent trading company focused on Ceylon tea exports, spices exports, fibre exports, sourcing, procurement, and market development.

The company provides international buyers and commercial partners with a structured point of contact for supply coordination, supplier review, documentation, pricing communication, and market-specific follow-up.
