Capabilities

Trading capabilities built for exports, sourcing, procurement, and market entry.

Our role is to create a clear, professional route between buyers, suppliers, manufacturers, and export markets that require accountable commercial follow-up.

Sourcing and Procurement

Supplier and manufacturer identification based on product requirements, quality expectations, commercial terms, and delivery needs.

Price and Contract Negotiation

Commercial negotiation support to help buyers secure appropriate pricing, payment structures, and supply terms from qualified sources.

Supplier Verification

Supplier legitimacy checks, documentation review, and quality-control coordination before goods are shipped.

Market Research and Expansion

Local market analysis covering demand, competitor activity, price positioning, buyer behavior, and entry opportunities in Sri Lanka.

Distribution Network Access

Support for manufacturers seeking local buyer access, project opportunities, channel communication, and structured commercial introductions.

Export Category Coordination

Commercial coordination for tea exports, spices exports, fibre exports, documentation follow-up, and destination-market communication.

Enquiry Process

A clear route from requirement to commercial response.

The process is intentionally simple so customers, buyers, suppliers, and manufacturers can submit commercial requirements without ambiguity.

01

Define the Requirement

Share the product category, target price range, quality expectations, quantity, destination market, and required timing.

02

Source or Assess the Market

We review supplier options, export pathways, buyer demand, competitor context, and relevant distribution opportunities.

03

Coordinate Commercial Follow-up

The requirement is routed through the appropriate supplier, buyer, export channel, or local market contact for structured response.
