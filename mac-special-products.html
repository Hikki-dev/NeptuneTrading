Tubes for Bearing Cages

Brass / ALCOP

Seamless tubes from 8–110mm OD precision-drawn for bearing cage manufacture. Available in multiple brass and ALCOP alloy families to suit different load and speed requirements.

Materials
63/37 Brass CuZn40Pb2 60/40 Brass ALCOP Alloys
Applications
  • Taper roller bearings and ball bearings
  • Cylindrical bearings for motors and pumps
  • Compressor and textile machinery bearings
  • Railway wagon bearing assemblies

Bend Quality Tubes for Faucets & Plumbing

CuZn / Arsenic

Arsenic-bearing CuZn alloy tubes supplied in annealed and stress-relieved condition with bright finish. 50-year durability rated. Available in round, square, rectangle, D-profile, and octagon profiles.

Materials
CuZn As-bearing Annealed Stress-relieved
Applications
  • Faucet bodies and valve components
  • Sanitary fixtures and bathroom fittings
  • Plumbing systems requiring soldering or brazing
  • Long-life residential and commercial water lines

Tubes for Shipbuilding & Marine

CuNi 90/10 · 70/30

Seamless CuNi tubes engineered for constant seawater immersion. CuNi 90/10 (C70600) and CuNi 70/30 (C71500) provide superior biofouling resistance and long service life in marine environments.

Materials
C70600 CuNi 90/10 C71500 CuNi 70/30
Applications
  • Seagoing vessel piping and heat exchangers
  • Offshore drilling rigs and FPSO platforms
  • Marine hardware and sea chest systems
  • Anti-biofouling applications in seawater

Tubes for Synchronizer Rings

High-Strength Brass

Precision tubes in high-strength manganese-aluminium brass alloys for synchronizer ring production in automotive transmissions. Engineered for highly stressed, high-cycle fatigue conditions.

Materials
CuZn36MnAl2Si1 CuZn37Mn3Al2PbSi1 CuZn30Mn8Al3Si1NiCr
Applications
  • Automotive manual transmission synchronizer rings
  • High-stress gear synchronization components
  • Heavy-duty vehicle and truck gearboxes
  • Precision machined ring blanks for OEM supply

Tubes for Fittings, Flanges & Elbows

Custom Spec

Customizable tube and pipe produced to exact client specifications for downstream fabrication into fittings, flanges, and elbows. Supplied to dimensional tolerances suited for machining and forming.

Materials
Brass Alloys Copper Alloys Client-specified
Applications
  • HVAC fittings and compression connectors
  • Drainage and plumbing elbows
  • Flanges for residential and commercial systems
  • Industrial pipe joining and flow control

Brass Tubes for Water-Lubricated Bearings

Naval / Leaded Brass

Naval brass and leaded brass tubes per international standards for water-lubricated bearing applications. Environmentally compliant with reduced maintenance requirements compared to oil-lubricated alternatives.

Materials
Naval Brass Leaded Brass Intl. Standards
Applications
  • Marine industry bearing solutions
  • Water-lubricated stern tube bearings
  • Environmentally sensitive marine operations
  • Low-maintenance shaft bearing liners
