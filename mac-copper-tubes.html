Key Properties & Advantages

MEDIFLOW-marked medical pipe pre-cleaned, nitrogen-filled, and sealed end-to-end

DHP grade is weld-compatible and hydrogen-embrittlement resistant for brazing

ETP grade provides maximum electrical and thermal conductivity for power applications

Finned heat exchanger tubes available in tension-wound, embedded, and extruded forms

Full traceability with heat number marking and test certificates per EN 10204

U-bend tube configurations available for shell-and-tube heat exchanger fabrication
