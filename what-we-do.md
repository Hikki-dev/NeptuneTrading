## Frequently Asked Questions

What does "product sourcing" mean at Neptune Trading?

Product sourcing means we identify, vet, and coordinate supply from international manufacturers on behalf of Sri Lankan buyers. We match your product specification, quantity, and compliance requirement to verified international suppliers and negotiate commercial terms including pricing and delivery timelines.

What export products does Neptune Trading coordinate?

We coordinate export development for Ceylon black and CTC tea, Ceylon cinnamon and spices, and coconut coir fibre, supported by CEC Commercial Export Company. Key export markets include the Middle East, South Asia, Malaysia, Singapore, and East Asia.

Can Neptune Trading help international manufacturers enter Sri Lanka?

Yes. We provide market entry and commercial agency services for international manufacturers seeking Sri Lankan public infrastructure bids, utility contracts, and private sector projects. This includes government tender navigation, import licensing, SLS certification coordination, and localized brand representation.

How does the Neptune Trading enquiry process work?

Submit your requirement with product category, quantity, timing, and documentation needs. Our desk reviews the scope, identifies the correct supply route, coordinates with the relevant manufacturer or principal, and returns a structured commercial response including pricing, availability, and documentation support.
