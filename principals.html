Principal Representation FAQ

What is principal representation in trading?

Principal representation means a local trading company acts as the official commercial agent for an international manufacturer in a specific country. Neptune Trading Company performs this role for HUMMER (in Sri Lanka & Maldives), and Metal Alloys Corporation in Sri Lanka — handling local enquiries, bid coordination, compliance documentation, and supply on behalf of each principal.

Can international suppliers partner with Neptune Trading?

Yes. International manufacturers looking for Sri Lanka market representation are welcome to contact Neptune Trading. We evaluate new partnerships based on product category, market fit, compliance standards, and commercial viability. Submit your company profile and product range through our contact form.

Does Neptune Trading support market entry in Sri Lanka?

Yes. We provide full market entry support including government tender registration, import licensing, SLS certification coordination, localized commercial representation, and structured bid coordination for public and private sector projects in Sri Lanka.

How can buyers enquire about represented products?

Submit a product enquiry through our contact form or email info@neptunetrading.lk. Include the product required, quantity, application, delivery location, technical specifications, and your company name for a structured commercial response from our trading desk.
