Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Neptune Trading Company (Pvt) Ltd. collects, handles, and protects information submitted through this website.

Data Collection

Information We Receive

When a visitor submits an enquiry form, Neptune Trading Company may receive the contact name, company name, email address, phone number, business area of interest, and custom message details supplied by the visitor.

Data Usage

How Information Is Used

Submitted information is used strictly to review and respond to export, sourcing, procurement, pricing, market development, and trading enquiries. It may be shared internally or with relevant suppliers, buyers, or service partners only to coordinate quotation details.

Retention

Data Retention

We retain enquiry records only for reasonable business follow-up, quotation bidding cycles, commercial procurement compliance, and historical communication reference.
