About Metal Alloys Corporation

Metal Alloys Corporation is a premier manufacturer of non-ferrous metal products, specializing in copper-based alloys for demanding industrial applications. Their range covers brass, bronze, and copper alloys in tube, rod, pipe, and sheet forms. Neptune Trading Company provides direct quotation channels, Mill Test Certificate (MTC) coordination, supply support, and local project bid coordination for the complete Metal Alloys range in Sri Lanka.

Quality Standards & Certifications
