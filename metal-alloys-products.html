Technical copper, brass, and bronze alloys for industrial, marine, and engineering applications. Neptune Trading Company is the authorized distributor for Metal Alloys Corporation in Sri Lanka.

About Metal Alloys Corporation

Metal Alloys Corporation is a premier manufacturer of non-ferrous metal products, specializing in copper-based alloys for demanding industrial applications. Their product range includes brass, bronze, and copper alloys in tube, rod, pipe, and sheet forms. Neptune Trading Company provides direct quotation channels, supply coordination, and local project bid support for the complete Metal Alloys range in Sri Lanka.

Brass Alloys

Typical Applications

  • Plumbing & sanitary fixture fittings
  • Precision CNC machined components
  • Aesthetic builder hardware & fixtures

Bronze Alloys

High-strength copper-tin, aluminium-bronze, and phosphor-bronze alloys. Designed for high load capacity, excellent anti-friction properties, and sea water resistance.

Typical Applications
  • Heavy-duty engine bushings & bearings
  • Marine propeller shafts & hardware
  • Industrial pump gears & wear plates
  • High-load hydraulic cylinder liners

Copper Alloys

Metallurgical Quality Assurance

Quality Standards & Certifications

Metal Alloys Corporation copper, brass, and bronze products are manufactured under rigorous quality systems, meeting strict global industrial specifications.

ISO 9001:2015 Quality

Produced in state-of-the-art smelting and extrusion facilities certified to ISO 9001:2015 quality management systems.

Global Standard Compliance

Fully compliant with ASTM (American), BS (British), DIN (German), JIS (Japanese), EN (European), and IS (Indian) metallurgical specifications.

Advanced Lab Testing

Smelts undergo optical emission spectrometry chemical analysis, eddy current defect testing, and mechanical tensile stress tests.

100% Traceability

Every commercial batch is supplied with an official EN 10204 3.1 Mill Test Certificate (MTC) detailing complete chemical and physical properties.
